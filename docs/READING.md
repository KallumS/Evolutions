# Reading notes: what the music rests on

The user's Educational Materials zip (uploaded again on 2026-10-21) is the
same collection Good Idea read in full; its notes are in Good Idea's
`docs/READING.md`, source by source. This is which of them Midi Evolver
uses, and where. Nothing here is a decision; the decisions are in
`decisions/`.

| Source | What it says | Here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hutchinson, *Music Theory for the 21st-Century Classroom*, ch. 35 (minimalism) | **Phase shifting** (Reich: loops of different lengths going out of step and back; a pattern rotated a note at a time). **Additive process** (Glass, *Two Pages*: a figure repeated, a note added or taken away; *Einstein on the Beach*: chords lengthened by added subdivisions). | The arpeggio's 5–7-note loop against a bar of 4; its turns, growing and shrinking (0004); its eighths → triplets → sixteenths; the pulse's rotating accents. |
| Hutchinson, ch. 14 (accompaniment textures) | Arpeggios over a bass; repeated eighths and quarters; afterbeats. | The pulse's repeated notes; the bass under the arpeggio. |
| Belkin, *Orchestration* | **Planes of tone**; background as *movement* (repeated notes, arpeggiation, one or two mildly varied motives, consistency, rests) or *resonance* (held notes); planes apart by register and rhythm; **crescendo by adding instruments in order**, something kept back for the climax; chords **wide at the bottom, close at the top**. | The five layers and the order they come in (0003); the pad's voicing; the pulse kept in eighths, with rests, until the top. |
| *Orchestration Analysis* | Register keeps elements apart; the bass an octave or more under; **doubling by displacement in time** (echo). | The layers' registers; the sparkle's echo. |
| Open Music Theory, embellishing tones | The **unaccented neighbour**: a step away and back, off the beat. | Colour tones in the arpeggio only on off-beat steps (0005). |
| Hutchinson ch. 32 (impressionism) | Added tones (9, 11, 13) as colour. | The colour tones themselves (0005). |
| The REAPER JSFX reference and API page | The language and the calls. | CLAUDE.md §1 and §4; every call checked there. |

From the family rather than the books: Midi Variator's clash guard (no new
semitone, major seventh or minor ninth against anything sounding) and its
feel (a few milliseconds of timing, a few steps of velocity); Good Idea's
"an idea is a number".
